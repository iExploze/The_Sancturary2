# The Sancturary

The Sancturary is a cooperative first-person escape-horror game set inside a mental asylum known as the Sancturary. The institution contains extremely strange, original monsters that were designed and confined there.

You and your friends are trapped inside. The goal is simple: survive and escape.

## Gameplay

Players must move through the Sancturary, hide from monsters, search rooms, find keys and hints, and solve environmental puzzles. Escaping depends on learning each level, using different movement mechanics, and knowing when to run, hide, or stop to investigate.

Small weapons can be found and picked up, but inventory space and ammunition are limited. Weapons provide a chance to survive; they do not turn the game into a combat-focused shooter.

When a player is killed, their friends may be able to revive them. If the group cannot recover, the level restarts from the latest checkpoint.

The game is planned around multiple levels. Each level introduces new challenges, puzzles, environments, and monsters. The lore has not been established yet; for now, every level is about surviving what is inside and finding a way out.

## Inspiration

The game is inspired by the pursuit and hiding of *Outlast* and the exploration, inventory pressure, and puzzles of *Resident Evil*.

## Development

The Sancturary is being developed in Unity 6.3 LTS using editor version 6000.3.15f1. It is a 3D reimagining of an unfinished 2D prototype created by Ian Zhang and Vlad Ign.
